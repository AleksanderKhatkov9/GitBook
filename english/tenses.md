# 4. Времена глагола

> [GrammarWay — Времена](https://grammarway.com/ru/) · [English Grammar in Use](https://www.hasanboy.uz/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/English-grammar-in-Use.pdf) · [English File](https://online.fliphtml5.com/aznks/ifwn/#p=1)

## Таблица всех времён (актив)

| Время | Утверждение | Вопрос | Отрицание |
|-------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Present Simple | I work / He works | Do you work? / Does he work? | I don't / He doesn't |
| Present Continuous | I am working | Are you working? | I'm not working |
| Present Perfect | I have worked | Have you worked? | I haven't worked |
| Present Perfect Continuous | I have been working | Have you been working? | I haven't been working |
| Past Simple | I worked / I went | Did you work? | I didn't work |
| Past Continuous | I was working | Were you working? | I wasn't working |
| Past Perfect | I had worked | Had you worked? | I hadn't worked |
| Past Perfect Continuous | I had been working | Had you been working? | I hadn't been working |
| Future Simple | I will work | Will you work? | I won't work |
| Future Continuous | I will be working | Will you be working? | I won't be working |
| Future Perfect | I will have worked | Will you have worked? | I won't have worked |
| Future Perfect Continuous | I will have been working | Will you have been…? | I won't have been… |

---

## Present Simple

**Когда:** факты, привычки, расписания, вечные истины.

Маркеры: *always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays*.

```
I work from home.
She works in an office.
Does he speak English? — Yes, he does.
The train leaves at 9.
```

---

## Present Continuous

**Когда:** действие сейчас / около сейчас; временная ситуация; планы (уже решённые).

Маркеры: *now, at the moment, today, this week, Look! Listen!*

```
I am reading now.
She is staying with friends this week.
We are meeting tomorrow at 6.
```

Не употребляются в Continuous (обычно): *know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, belong* (stative verbs).

---

## Present Perfect

**Когда:** опыт; результат в настоящем; действие началось в прошлом и важно сейчас; *just / already / yet / ever / never / since / for*.

```
I have visited London.          ← опыт
She has lost her keys.          ← результат: ключей нет
We have lived here for 5 years.
Have you ever tried sushi?
I haven't finished yet.
```

**Present Perfect ≠ Past Simple:**  
*I have lost my keys* (ещё не нашёл) vs *I lost my keys yesterday* (факт в прошлом + время).

---

## Present Perfect Continuous

**Когда:** длительность до сейчас; временный процесс с акцентом на продолжительность.

```
I have been working all day.
It has been raining since morning.
```

---

## Past Simple

**Когда:** завершённое действие в прошлом + указание времени.

Маркеры: *yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago, when*.

```
I worked yesterday.
She went to Rome last year.
Did you see that film? — No, I didn't.
```

---

## Past Continuous

**Когда:** процесс в определённый момент прошлого; фон для другого действия.

```
I was working at 8 p.m.
While I was cooking, the phone rang.
```

---

## Past Perfect

**Когда:** действие **до** другого прошлого (= «раньше прошлого»).

```
When I arrived, the train had left.
I had never seen such a bug before that day.
```

---

## Past Perfect Continuous

**Когда:** длительность до момента в прошлом.

```
He had been coding for hours before the demo.
```

---

## Future Simple (will)

**Когда:** решение в момент речи; прогноз; обещание; предложение.

```
I'll help you.
I think it will rain.
Will you open the window?
```

---

## be going to

См. [Обороты](constructions.md): планы и очевидные прогнозы.

```
I'm going to learn TypeScript.
Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.
```

---

## Future Continuous / Perfect

```
This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Berlin.     ← Future Continuous
By Friday I'll have finished the report.         ← Future Perfect
By June I'll have been working here for a year.  ← Future Perfect Continuous
```

---

## Future in the Past

Будущее относительно прошлого: *would*, *was going to*, *was to*.

```
She said she would call.
I thought it was going to rain.
```

---

## Пассивный залог (Passive)

**Формула:** *be* (в нужном времени) + **V3**.

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| They write code. | Code **is written**. |
| They wrote code. | Code **was written**. |
| They have written code. | Code **has been written**. |
| They will write code. | Code **will be written**. |

```
The bug was fixed yesterday.
The API is used by millions of apps.
```

**Have something done** — услуга / кто-то делает за вас:

```
I had my laptop repaired.
She is having her hair cut.
```

---

## Согласование времён (Sequence of Tenses)

В косвенной речи после прошедшего в главном:

| Прямая речь | Косвенная |
|-------------|-----------|
| Present → | Past |
| Past → | Past Perfect |
| will → | would |
| can → | could |

```
"I work here." → She said she worked there.
"I will go." → He said he would go.
```
