# 4. Времена глагола

> [GrammarWay — Времена](https://grammarway.com/ru/) · [Таблица всех времён](https://grammarway.com/ru/all-verb-tenses/) · [English Grammar in Use](https://www.hasanboy.uz/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/English-grammar-in-Use.pdf) · [English File](https://online.fliphtml5.com/aznks/ifwn/#p=1)

В английском 12 основных времён активного залога: **3 времени** (Present / Past / Future) × **4 аспекта** (Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous).

| Аспект | Смысл |
|--------|--------|
| **Simple** | факт, привычка, завершённое событие |
| **Continuous** | процесс в конкретный момент |
| **Perfect** | результат / «раньше» относительно точки отсчёта |
| **Perfect Continuous** | длительность до точки отсчёта |

---

## Как читать формулы

| Обозначение | Что это |
|-------------|---------|
| **V / V1** | первая форма (инфинитив без *to*): *work, go* |
| **Vs** | V + *-s/-es* для he/she/it: *works, goes* |
| **V2** | Past Simple: *worked* или 2-я форма неправильного (*went*) |
| **V3** | Past Participle: *worked* или 3-я форма (*gone, written*) |
| **V-ing** | *working, going* |
| **S** | подлежащее |
| **QW** | вопросительное слово (*what, where, when…*) |

Краткая таблица неправильных глаголов — в [Глаголах](verbs.md).

## Present Simple

**Формула:** `S + V / Vs`

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|--|---------------------|---------------|
| + | I **work** | He **works** |
| − | I **don't work** | He **doesn't work** |
| ? | **Do** you **work**? | **Does** he **work**? |

После *do / does / don't / doesn't* смысловой глагол всегда в **V1** (без *-s*).

### Окончание -s / -es (he/she/it)

| Правило | Примеры |
|---------|---------|
| Обычно + *-s* | work → works, read → reads |
| -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o → *-es* | pass → passes, watch → watches, go → goes |
| согласная + y → *-ies* | study → studies, try → tries |
| гласная + y → *-s* | play → plays |
| особые | have → has, be → is |

### To be в Present Simple

*To be* не берёт *do/does*: формы **am / is / are**.

```
I am a developer. / I'm not ready.
She is at the office. / She isn't here.
Are you free? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
```

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Общие факты, вкусы, мнения | She **likes** TypeScript. |
| Вечные истины, научные факты | Water **boils** at 100°C. |
| Привычки и регулярные действия | I **deploy** on Fridays. |
| Расписание (даже про будущее) | The train **leaves** at 9. The meeting **starts** at 10. |
| Инструкции, рецепты | First you **clone** the repo. Then you **run** `npm install`. |
| Комментарий «прямо сейчас» (спорт, демо) | He **clicks** Deploy and the build **starts**. |
| Stative verbs вместо Continuous | I **know** the answer. She **needs** help. |
| Заголовки | Company **opens** a new office in Berlin. |

### Маркеры

*always, usually, often, regularly, sometimes, seldom, rarely, never*  
*every day / week / month, twice a week, from time to time*  
*on Mondays, at the weekend, at 7 o'clock* (расписание)

```
I work from home.
She works in an office.
Does he speak English? — Yes, he does.
How often do you review PRs?
The train leaves at 9.
Nobody knows the root cause.   ← отрицательное слово, глагол без don't
```

## Present Continuous

**Формула:** `am / is / are + V-ing`

| | I | he / she / it | you / we / they |
|--|---|---------------|-----------------|
| + | I **am working** | She **is working** | They **are working** |
| − | I'm **not working** | She **isn't working** | They **aren't working** |
| ? | **Am** I **working**? | **Is** she **working**? | **Are** they **working**? |

Сокращения: *I'm, he's, she's, it's, you're, we're, they're*.

### Окончание -ing (кратко)

| Правило | Примеры |
|---------|---------|
| обычно + *-ing* | work → working |
| немое *-e* отбрасывается | write → writing, take → taking |
| *-ie* → *-ying* | lie → lying |
| краткая ударная гласная + согласная → удвоение | run → running, stop → stopping |
| *-ee* сохраняется | see → seeing, agree → agreeing |

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Действие **прямо сейчас** | I **am reading** the logs. |
| Временная ситуация *around now* | She **is staying** with friends this week. |
| Зафиксированный план (время/место известны) | We **are meeting** tomorrow at 6. |
| Меняющаяся ситуация | The codebase **is growing** fast. |
| Раздражение + *always / constantly* | He **is always interrupting** me. |

### Маркеры

*now, right now, still, currently, at the moment, at present, today, tonight, this morning, these days, nowadays*

```
I am reading now.
She is staying with friends this week.
We are meeting tomorrow at 6.
Look! The build is failing.
You're always pushing without tests.
```

### Stative verbs (обычно не Continuous)

Состояние, а не процесс: *know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, belong, own, seem, prefer, remember, forget, mean*.

```
I know the answer.     ✓
I am knowing…          ✗
I need help right now. ✓
```

Часть глаголов меняет смысл: *I'm thinking about it* (обдумываю) vs *I think it's a bug* (мнение).

## Present Perfect

**Формула:** `have / has + V3`

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|--|---------------------|---------------|
| + | I **have worked** | She **has worked** |
| − | I **haven't worked** | She **hasn't worked** |
| ? | **Have** you **worked**? | **Has** she **worked**? |

Сокращения: *I've, you've, we've, they've, he's, she's, it's* (здесь *'s* = *has*).

Правильные глаголы: V3 = V + *-ed*. Неправильные: 3-я форма (*written, gone, done*).

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Результат важен **сейчас**, точное время не важно | She **has lost** her keys. (ключей нет) |
| Опыт жизни (*ever / never / once*) | **Have** you **ever tried** sushi? |
| Незавершённый период: *today, this week, this year* | I **have written** three tickets this morning. (утро ещё идёт) |
| Действие началось в прошлом и продолжается (*for / since*) | We **have lived** here for 5 years. |
| *just / already / yet / so far / lately* | I **haven't finished** yet. |

### Маркеры

*ever, never, already, yet, not yet, just, so far, lately, recently*  
*once, many times, several times, before, today*  
*this week / month / year / afternoon*  
*for an hour, for a long time* · *since 12 o'clock, since April*

*already* — обычно в утверждении; *yet* — в вопросах и отрицаниях.

```
I have visited London.                 ← опыт
She has lost her keys.                 ← результат: ключей нет
We have lived here for 5 years.
Have you ever tried sushi?
I haven't finished yet.
He's just pushed the fix.
```

### Present Perfect ≠ Past Simple

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|-----------------|-------------|
| связь с настоящим, время **не** названо точно | факт в прошлом + **когда** |
| *I have lost my keys.* (ещё не нашёл) | *I lost my keys yesterday.* |
| *Have you seen this film?* (опыт) | *Did you see it last night?* |
| *I've worked here since 2020.* (до сих пор) | *I worked there in 2019.* (уже не работаю) |

## Present Perfect Continuous

**Формула:** `have / has + been + V-ing`

| + | I **have been working** |
| − | I **haven't been working** |
| ? | **Have** you **been working**? |

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Началось в прошлом, **ещё идёт**, важен процесс | I **have been working** all day. |
| Длительность до сейчас (*for / since*) | It **has been raining** since morning. |
| Недавняя активность, результат виден | I'm tired — I **have been debugging** for hours. |
| Раздражение из‑за длящейся ситуации | He **has been calling** me all morning. |

### Маркеры

*all day / morning / night, for 2 hours, since 10 o'clock, since yesterday, since 2020, lately, recently*

### Perfect vs Perfect Continuous

| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|-----------------|----------------------------|
| акцент на **результат / количество** | акцент на **длительность / процесс** |
| *I have written three emails.* | *I have been writing emails all morning.* |
| *She has lived here for 5 years.* (факт) | *She has been living here for 5 years.* (всё ещё, процесс) |

Со stative verbs (*know, have* в значении «иметь») — обычно Perfect, не Continuous: *I've known her for years.*

```
I have been working all day.
It has been raining since morning.
How long have you been waiting?
```

## Past Simple

**Формула:** `S + V2`  ·  вопрос/отрицание: `did + V1`

| + | I **worked** / I **went** |
| − | I **didn't work** / I **didn't go** |
| ? | **Did** you **work**? / **Did** you **go**? |

После *did / didn't* глагол **без** *-ed* и не во 2-й форме: *Did you go?* (не *Did you went?*).

Форма прошедшего **одинакова** для всех лиц, кроме *to be*.

### To be в Past Simple

| | I / he / she / it | you / we / they |
|--|-------------------|-----------------|
| + | I **was** | We **were** |
| − | I **wasn't** | We **weren't** |
| ? | **Was** she…? | **Were** you…? |

```
She was angry yesterday.
Were they at the standup? — No, they weren't.
```

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Завершённое действие + **когда** (явно или подразумевается) | I **met** her yesterday. |
| Ситуация в прошлом, сейчас уже не так | He **was** a famous singer. She **worked** abroad for 20 years. |
| Событие, которое больше не повторится | I **won** that contest when I was a student. |
| Цепочка действий одно за другим | I **woke up**, **opened** the laptop and **checked** Slack. |

Одновременные процессы в прошлом — [Past Continuous](#past-continuous).

### Маркеры

*yesterday, the day before yesterday, just now, the other day*  
*last week / month / year, an hour ago, two weeks ago*  
*in 2019, at 6 o'clock, when I was a child*

```
I worked yesterday.
She went to Rome last year.
Did you see that film? — No, I didn't.
Where did she work?
He closed the laptop, grabbed his keys and left.
```

## Past Continuous

**Формула:** `was / were + V-ing`

| | I / he / she / it | you / we / they |
|--|-------------------|-----------------|
| + | I **was working** | They **were working** |
| − | I **wasn't working** | They **weren't working** |
| ? | **Was** he **working**? | **Were** you **working**? |

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Процесс в **конкретный момент** прошлого | I **was working** at 8 p.m. |
| Фон для короткого действия (Past Simple) | While I **was cooking**, the phone **rang**. |
| Два процесса одновременно | Ann **was cleaning** while Mark **was washing** the car. |
| Неодобрение (*always*) | He **was always losing** the keys. |
| План, который не состоялся (*was going to*) | I **was going to** call you, but my phone died. |

### Маркеры

*while, when, as, all morning / day / night, last Monday, at that moment, at the same time, at 6 o'clock yesterday*

```
I was working at 8 p.m.
While I was cooking, the phone rang.
What were you doing when the server crashed?
```

## Past Perfect

**Формула:** `had + V3`  (для всех лиц)

| + | I **had worked** / She **had gone** |
| − | I **hadn't worked** |
| ? | **Had** you **worked**? |

Это «**раньше прошлого**»: сначала одно действие, потом другое (тоже в прошлом).

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Действие **до** другого прошлого | When I arrived, the train **had left**. |
| Результат, уже видимый в прошлом | The room was empty — everyone **had gone**. |
| *hardly… when / no sooner… than* | I **had hardly sat down** when Slack pinged. |

### Маркеры

*before, after, already, just, never, by two o'clock, by that time, by evening, no sooner… than, hardly… when*

```
When I arrived, the train had left.
I had never seen such a bug before that day.
By the time the demo started, she had finished the slides.
```

Если события просто идут по порядку и время ясно — достаточно Past Simple: *I opened the door and went in.*

---

## Past Perfect Continuous

**Формула:** `had been + V-ing`

| + | He **had been coding** |
| − | He **hadn't been coding** |
| ? | **Had** he **been coding**? |

### Когда использовать

Длительность **до** момента в прошлом: сколько уже шло действие, когда случилось другое.

### Маркеры

*before, till / until, all day / year, for an hour, for a day, since 5 o'clock, since 1991, since last week*

```
He had been coding for hours before the demo.
They had been waiting for 40 minutes when the bus arrived.
```

## Future Simple (*will*)

**Формула:** `will + V`

В современном английском **will** со всеми лицами. *Shall* с *I / we* встречается в старых текстах и формальных вопросах (*Shall we start?*).

| + | I **will work** / I'll **work** |
| − | I **won't work** (*will not*) |
| ? | **Will** you **work**? |

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Факт / последовательность в будущем | He **will come** tomorrow. |
| Прогноз, мнение (*I think, probably, I'm sure*) | I think it **will rain**. |
| Обещание, надежда, угроза (*promise, hope, swear*) | I **promise** I'll **help**. |
| Неизбежное будущее | Dawn **will come**. |
| **Спонтанное** решение в момент речи | The deploy failed. — I'll **check** the logs. |

### Маркеры

*tomorrow, tonight, later, the day after tomorrow, soon, as soon as*  
*next week / year, in a week, in two days*

```
I'll help you.
I think it will rain.
Will you open the window?
Probably they will find a workaround.
```

*Will* не ставят в придаточных времени после *if, when, as soon as, until*: *I'll call you when I **finish*** (не *when I will finish*).

## be going to

Подробнее — [Обороты](constructions.md). Кратко:

**Формула:** `am / is / are + going to + V`

| *will* | *going to* |
|--------|------------|
| решение **сейчас** | план **уже был** |
| мнение / обещание | очевидный исход по признакам |

```
I'm going to learn TypeScript.
Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.
I've booked a ticket. I'm going to fly on Monday.
```

Past: *was / were going to* — собирался, но не вышло.

## Future Continuous

**Формула:** `will be + V-ing`

| + | I **will be flying** |
| − | I **won't be flying** |
| ? | **Will** you **be flying**? |

### Когда использовать

| Случай | Пример |
|--------|--------|
| Процесс в конкретный момент будущего | This time tomorrow I'll **be flying** to Berlin. |
| Два процесса одновременно в будущем | I'll **be coding** while she **is** in the meeting. |
| Действие «как обычно случится» / вежливый вопрос о планах | **Will** you **be using** the staging server this afternoon? |

### Маркеры

*this time tomorrow, at 5 o'clock, at that moment, all night, from 10 to 11, during the day*

```
This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Berlin.
Don't call at 9 — I'll be presenting.
Will you be staying for the retro?
```

## Future Perfect

**Формула:** `will have + V3`

| + | I **will have finished** |
| − | I **won't have finished** |
| ? | **Will** you **have finished**? |

### Когда использовать

К **моменту в будущем** действие **уже будет завершено**.

### Маркеры

*by then, by the time, by 5 o'clock, by tomorrow, by next week, before, until*

```
By Friday I'll have finished the report.
By the time you arrive, we will have deployed.
```

## Future Perfect Continuous

**Формула:** `will have been + V-ing`

| + | I **will have been working** |
| − | I **won't have been working** |
| ? | **Will** you **have been working**? |

### Когда использовать

К моменту в будущем действие **уже будет длиться** какое-то время. Акцент на продолжительности.

### Маркеры

*for 2 hours / 1 year, till / until, by the end of the day / year*

```
By June I'll have been working here for a year.
By 6 p.m. she will have been debugging for three hours.
```

## Будущее: какую форму выбрать

| Форма | Когда |
|-------|--------|
| **will** | решение сейчас, прогноз, обещание |
| **going to** | намерение / очевидный исход |
| **Present Continuous** | договорённость: время и место уже есть |
| **Present Simple** | расписание |
| **Future Continuous** | процесс в точке будущего |
| **Future Perfect** | «уже будет сделано к…» |

```
I'll help.                          ← сейчас решил
I'm going to rewrite the module.    ← план
We're meeting at 10.                ← в календаре
The train leaves at 9.              ← расписание
I'll be flying at noon.             ← процесс
I'll have finished by Friday.       ← результат к сроку
```

## Future in the Past

Будущее **с точки зрения прошлого**: в косвенной речи и рассказе о прошлых планах.

*will → would* (реже *shall → should*). Четыре формы, как у Future:

| Форма | Active | Пример |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Simple | *would + V* | She said she **would call**. |
| Continuous | *would be + V-ing* | He wrote he **would be working** on Monday. |
| Perfect | *would have + V3* | I hoped we **would have finished** by Friday. |
| Perfect Continuous | *would have been + V-ing* | They knew they **would have been living** there for 10 years. |

Пассив: *would be + V3*, *would have been + V3*. Continuous / Perfect Continuous в пассиве обычно не используют.

Отрицание: *wouldn't*. Вопрос: *Would you…?* В придаточных — по правилам косвенной речи.

```
She said she would call.
I thought it was going to rain.
He hoped the work would be done soon.
```

## Пассивный залог (Passive)

**Смысл:** подлежащее **не делает** действие, а **подвергается** ему. Формула: **форма *be* + V3**. Только переходные глаголы (есть дополнение).

Действующее лицо — через *by*, часто опускают (не важно / неизвестно / очевидно).

**Нет пассива** у Future Continuous и всех Perfect Continuous.

| Время | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Present Simple | They write code. | Code **is written**. |
| Past Simple | They wrote code. | Code **was written**. |
| Future Simple | They will write code. | Code **will be written**. |
| Present Continuous | They are writing it. | It **is being written**. |
| Past Continuous | They were writing it. | It **was being written**. |
| Present Perfect | They have written it. | It **has been written**. |
| Past Perfect | They had written it. | It **had been written**. |
| Future Perfect | They will have written it. | It **will have been written**. |
| Future in the Past | They would write it. | It **would be written**. |
| going to | They are going to write it. | It **is going to be written**. |

Вопрос: *be* (или *will / have*) вперёд: *Was the bug fixed? Has the API been updated?*

```
The bug was fixed yesterday.
The API is used by millions of apps.
The letter is being written right now.
The package has already been delivered.
```

## Have something done

Кто-то делает действие **за вас** (услуга) или с вами что-то случилось. Меняется только *have*; V3 стоит **после** объекта.

**Формула:** `have + объект + V3`

| Время | Пример |
|-------|--------|
| Present Simple | I **have** my laptop **repaired** every year. |
| Present Continuous | She **is having** her hair **cut**. |
| Past Simple | I **had** my laptop **repaired**. |
| Present Perfect | We **have had** the office **painted**. |
| Future / going to | I'll **have** the lock **changed**. / I'm **going to have** it **fixed**. |
| Modal | You **should have** this **checked**. |

Порядок важен: *I had my laptop repaired* (услуга) ≠ *I had repaired my laptop* (Past Perfect, сделал сам).

```
I had my laptop repaired.
She is having her hair cut.
We need to have the SSL certificate renewed.
```

## Согласование времён (Sequence of Tenses)

Если в **главном** предложении прошедшее время, время в придаточном сдвигается «на шаг назад» (или в Future in the Past). В русском такого правила нет.

| Прямая речь | Косвенная (после *said / thought*) |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| Present Simple / Continuous | Past Simple / Continuous |
| Present Perfect / Past Simple | Past Perfect |
| Past Perfect | Past Perfect (без сдвига) |
| will | would |
| can / may | could / might |
| must | often **had to** |
| could, might, should, would, ought to | не меняются |

### Одновременно с главным → Past Simple / Continuous

```
"I work here." → She said she worked there.
I was sure Kate was busy that day.
```

### Раньше главного → Past Perfect (длительность → Past Perfect Continuous)

```
I thought you had finished.
She said she had been playing the guitar all day.
```

### Позже главного → Future in the Past

```
"I will go." → He said he would go.
You said you would be waiting for me.
```

Если главное в **Present / Future** — согласования нет: *She says she went shopping yesterday.*

**Исключения (сдвиг не обязателен):** вечные истины, привычки, расписание; придаточные причины / сравнения / определительные, если смысл требует настоящего.

```
The students knew that water consists of hydrogen and oxygen.
I asked when the first train usually leaves.
```

Указательные слова часто сдвигаются: *now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there, this → that*.

Подробнее о косвенной речи — [Предложение](sentence.md).

## Что учить в первую очередь

Для уровня A2–B1 достаточно уверенно держать:

1. Present Simple / Continuous  
2. Past Simple (+ частые неправильные)  
3. Present Perfect (*ever / never / just / already / yet / for / since*)  
4. *will* vs *going to*  
5. Passive: Present и Past Simple  

Остальные Perfect / Continuous — когда базовые четыре уже не путаются.
